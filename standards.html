Label both ends.

These are the standards Jack MacKinnon works to. They apply to the room and to the software. The desk is part of the system.

  1. Label both ends. The name at the device matches the name at the port.
  2. Trace the cable. You can follow it without unplugging the room.
  3. Clear the desk in one pass. Daily tools stay in reach. The cable well is not a drawer.
  4. Keep every mount inside the edge of the desk. Test the swing before you load the tray.
  5. Light the task with one pool. Keep glare off the panel and out of the eyes.
  6. Write software that uses those names, that screen count, and that clear surface.
  7. Mark a drawing as an illustration when it shows a principle. A principle is not a record of a finished room.
