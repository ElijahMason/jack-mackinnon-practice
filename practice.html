What he builds.

Jack MacKinnon takes the physical layer upstream of the software: the office as it is actually used, then the code that expects that office. The desk is part of the system.

01 Layout
Where the bench sits, what stays on the top, and what has a route off it.
02 Mounts
Arms and trays that keep the panel inside the desk edge and the brick off the top.
03 Lighting
One pool on the task. Out of the eyes. Off a full-panel reflection.
04 Cable routes
A path you can draw, pull, and trace. Both ends labeled with the same name.
05 Workflow
How a request moves from the person, across the desk, through the software, and back.
06 Software
Programs for those same teams. The tool reads the labels. It does not invent them.

He builds and installs the room, and he writes the software. The plates on this site show the principles. They are not a record of a finished room.
